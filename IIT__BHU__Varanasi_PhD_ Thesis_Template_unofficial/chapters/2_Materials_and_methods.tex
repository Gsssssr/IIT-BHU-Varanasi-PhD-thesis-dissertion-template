\section{Statistical Analysis}
\label{Statistical}
ANOVA and Statistical analysis was performed using OriginPro software.
In order to determine the statistical significance of differences between treatment groups (Control, VEGF, Anti-VEGF, and Hydrocortisone) one-way analysis of variance (ANOVA) was conducted.
Tukey’s HSD post-hoc test was applied to pairwise comparisons for identification of differences between the groups at the significance level \textcolor{black}{of} 0.05.
Results are reported as statistically significant, where the significance levels are denoted as follows: ns ($p > 0.05$): Not significant, * ($p \leq 0.05$): Significant, ** ($p \leq 0.01$): Very significant, and *** ($p \leq 0.001$): Highly significant.
